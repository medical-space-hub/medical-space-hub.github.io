About this 3D anatomy atlas

Models use actual anatomical mesh data, not artificial geometric replacements. The male source is Z-Anatomy/BodyParts3D-derived geometry; the female source is a partial HuBMAP Human Reference Atlas reference and does not contain a complete female muscular or nervous system.

Every indexed structure has a unique identity and source metadata. Additional functions, vessels and clinical details appear only when a reference card exists. Missing descriptions are explicitly marked.

Male anatomical assets: CC BY-SA 4.0. Female assets: CC BY 4.0. File-level attribution and licences are included in this project. Educational use only.
